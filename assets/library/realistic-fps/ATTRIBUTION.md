Project modifications: WebP images transcoded to PNG; meshopt buffers decoded; quantized attributes converted to floating point; duplicate subasset names disambiguated; native ForgeAX import, viewmodel framing and runtime action adaptation. No upstream scripts executed. Immutable downloaded GLBs retained in `.assetlib/originals/realistic-fps/`. No Fab animation files downloaded or used.

Sleeve meshes were reshaped in bind pose to widen the forearms and soften the corners of a more rectangular silhouette. Original textures and skin weights were retained.
